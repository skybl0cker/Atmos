Atmos — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 9, 2026

Atmos is a weather app by Cash Egley. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: Atmos has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Your data stays on your device.

Location

Atmos requests your device location to show weather, radar, and alerts for where you are. Your coordinates are sent to third-party weather data providers (including Open-Meteo, the U.S. National Weather Service, RainViewer, and the National Hurricane Center) solely to fetch the forecast and radar imagery you asked to see. Your location is never sold, never used for advertising, and never stored on any server operated by us — there are none.

On-device storage

Your preferences (units, saved places, notification settings) are stored only on your device using the platform's local storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you enable weather alerts, they are generated on your device. No personal data leaves the device for this feature.

Third-party services

Atmos loads map tiles, radar imagery, and forecast data from third-party providers. Those providers receive the technical data needed to serve the request (such as map tile coordinates or a location query), subject to their own privacy policies.

Children

Atmos does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
