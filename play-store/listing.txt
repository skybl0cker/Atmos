Radar-first weather: live radar, 14-day forecast, alerts & storm tracking.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Atmos is a radar-first weather app for people who actually watch the sky.

LIVE RADAR, PREDICTED
- Animated radar with observed frames, short-term nowcast, and motion-extrapolated
  +30/+60 minute projections measured from real echo movement
- HRRR model "future radar" out to +6 hours, badged as model guidance

SEVERE WEATHER SITUATION ROOM
- A dedicated tab that follows the biggest threat: hurricanes, tornado
  outbreaks, flash floods, winter storms — and major earthquakes
- Threats ranked by distance from you; the closest gets live stats, a track
  map, warning polygons, and the latest news coverage
- Active tropical cyclones with NHC best-track position, intensity, and
  movement, even when official warnings lapse

FORECASTS & ALERTS
- 14-day daily forecast with highs, lows, and precipitation
- NWS alerts with official safety instructions
- Rain-start notifications

BUILT FOR ACCURACY
- Data from the National Weather Service, National Hurricane Center, USGS,
  Open-Meteo, and RainViewer
- Model output is always labeled as model output — no black boxes

No accounts. No ads. No analytics. Your location never leaves your device
except to fetch the weather you asked for.
